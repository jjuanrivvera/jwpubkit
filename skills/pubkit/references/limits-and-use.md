# Limits and personal use

- Nothing is bundled: results come only from what the user synced on their own machine.
- `sync`, `catalog` and subtitle retrieval depend on jw.org being reachable.
- JWPUB layouts differ between publication generations; an unsupported schema or missing media
  returns fewer fields rather than guessed ones.
- The media catalog covers what the official category tree and subtitle URLs expose.
- `backup annotate` is experimental; check its result visually in JW Library.
- Publication content belongs to its owner. pubkit is an independent tool for the user's own
  study. Do not republish extracted text, images or transcripts, do not commit them to a
  repository, and do not present your own wording as publication text.
